## Brand & Style

This design system reflects a curated intersection of contemporary youth streetwear and regal calm. Grounded in the philosophy of "confidence meets comfort," the aesthetic departs from aggressive, raw streetwear tropes in favor of warm, quiet assurance and elevated craftsmanship. It serves kids, women, and men seeking premium staples—primarily tailored tees, oversized heavyweight hoodies, and relaxed luxury loungewear.

The visual direction leans into **Refined Minimalist Luxury with Warm Heritage Touches**. Generous off-white breathing room, fine hairline metallic dividers, soft tactile cards, and delicate serif headlining present an editorial experience akin to an upscale fashion atelier, yet anchored by the effortless ease of everyday streetwear.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intimate, regal warmth:

- **Primary Burgundy (`#6D1E2C`)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, key headers, active states, and grounding structural branding elements.
- **Secondary Beige (`#EFE6DA`)**: Used for soft card backdrops, muted tag pills, surface containers, and tonal separation.
- **Accent Warm Gold (`#C9A24B`)**: Applied sparingly to elevate key highlights, badging, crest motifs, decorative rules, and premium micro-interactions.
- **Neutral Off-White/Cream (`#FAF6F0`)**: The ambient baseline surface, replacing harsh stark whites to induce relaxation and warmth.

For dark tones and text, rely on deep burgundy-tinted espresso (`#2A0C12`) rather than pure black to preserve organic richness. Dividers leverage 1px rules tinted with either 20% opacity primary burgundy or brushed warm gold.

## Typography

The typography couples an elegant, calligraphic serif headlining aesthetic with a soft, readable modern geometric sans-serif for functional UI.

- **Headlines & Display**: Rendered with fluid serif forms to echo timeless fashion editorial layouts and the brand's regal crest heritage.
- **Body & Labels**: Built with an approachable, contemporary geometric sans-serif featuring generous x-height and gentle terminal rounds. All caps navigation links, categories, and sub-headers utilize extended tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury catalog styling.

## Layout & Spacing

A relaxed 12-column grid anchors desktop views (max content width 1360px), shifting to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous whitespace (`space-2xl` section dividers and `margin` canvas framing) allows product photography—t-shirts, hoodies, and streetwear looks—to command focus without clutter. Editorial lookbook blocks alternate between asymmetrical single-column focal points and structured two-to-four column product matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy favors layered organic surfaces over harsh, artificial drop shadows:

- **Baseline Surfaces**: Cream (`#FAF6F0`) forms the underlying canvas. Secondary Beige (`#EFE6DA`) steps forward as container backdrops and floating panel cards.
- **Ambient Soft Shadows**: When elevation is necessary (e.g., sticky navigation bars, quick-buy drawers, modal overlays), use an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(109, 30, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Dividers & Rules**: Hairline 1px borders using Primary Burgundy at 15% opacity or Warm Gold (`#C9A24B`) at 40% opacity structure content softly without visual friction.

## Shapes

The design uses soft, subtle rounding (`roundedness: 1` corresponding to `0.25rem` / 4px on small interactive elements, up to `0.5rem` / 8px on product cards and lookbook panels). This maintains tailoring structure reminiscent of folded textiles and clean garment tags, softening harsh corners without entering bubbly or informal territory.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Deep burgundy fill (`#6D1E2C`), off-white typography (`#FAF6F0`), subtle 4px radius, padded with `space-sm` vertical and `space-lg` horizontal. Hover introduces gentle gold-tinted glow or a shift to deep wine.
  - *Secondary / Ghost*: Clear background, 1px Primary Burgundy or Warm Gold outline, uppercase tracking, transitioning to `#EFE6DA` fill on hover.
  - *Pill / Floating*: Reserved strictly for quick-add cart triggers and category filter badges.
- **Product Cards**:
  - Borderless `#EFE6DA` or muted cream card surfaces with an 8px radius. Product imagery is set against clean studio backdrops. Typography underneath features serif product titles and crisp sans-serif price tags.
- **Chips & Filters**:
  - Soft beige containers (`#EFE6DA`) with 4px corner radii, switching to burgundy fill with cream text when active.
- **Inputs & Fields**:
  - Understated baseline or soft pill-stroke inputs with a 1px border (`rgba(109, 30, 44, 0.2)`). Focus states smoothly shift the stroke to Warm Gold (`#C9A24B`) with no harsh browser rings.
- **Dividers & Badges**:
  - Section dividers leverage ornamental tapered hairpins with central gold orbs, evoking regal crest symmetry. Small collection tags ("NEW SEASON", "KIDS", "LIMITED DROP") appear in uppercase tracked typography with delicate gold borders.
