Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding materials, sizing, and founder-direct delivery.

We exclusively knit our textiles from combed 100% long-staple organic cotton at an intentional weight of 380 GSM for our fleece and 280 GSM for our tees. This achieves a sculptured drape that retains its silhouette without feeling stiff or abrasive against the skin.
Our adult silhouettes are patterned with an intentional boxy drop-shoulder streetwear fit. For a tailored, closer silhouette, we suggest sizing down one step. For youth and kids, our garments are pre-shrunk and true-to-age with generous room for comfortable movement.
For metropolitan New York orders, our founder personally oversees door-to-door hand delivery in signature Diva cotton tote garment boxes. National and international orders are personally hand-packed, tissue-wrapped with wax seal stamps, and dispatched within 24 hours.
Wash inside-out with cold water on a delicate cycle using mild detergent. Avoid bleach or heavy fabric softeners. We recommend flat air drying to preserve the rich botanical burgundy dyes and heavyweight weave structure for years of daily wear.
